Epic Report Generator builds PDF status reports from your Jira Cloud Epics. Connect with a Jira API token, pick the Epics or labels you care about, and it generates a landscape PDF with progress bars, a Gantt-style timeline, and per-Epic trend charts. Everything runs locally on your Mac.

FEATURES
• Connect to Jira Cloud with an API token (site URL, email, token)
• Report on a single Epic, or group several Epics under a label
• Bottom-up progress across Epics, stories, and subtasks
• Estimate by story points or by time in days
• Gantt-style timeline you can pin to a fixed date window
• Per-Epic trend charts: velocity, cycle time, scope change, and forecast
• Scope-certainty indicators, with per-item overrides
• Drag to reorder items; rename or hide individual children
• Set your own accent colour and fonts for both the app and the PDF
• Light and dark themes
• Export a 16:9 landscape PDF, ready to drop into a deck

Your Jira credentials live in the macOS Keychain and are used only to reach your own Jira site. The app collects no account or usage data.
